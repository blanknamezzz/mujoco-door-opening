\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We implemented a continuous mobile door-opening system in which inspection,
positioning, and grasp selection learn from downstream physical outcomes.
Current-grasp-conditioned station labels and fixed-continuation inspection
targets connect the decisions through staged LoRA and task-head training.
On the latest test, the selected policy achieves $70/72$
correct decisions and matches always-inspect task outcomes with 43 rather
than 72 inspections and 38.31 rather than 55.68 simulated seconds per episode.
Three disjoint cohorts yield $210/216$ correct decisions. The next priorities
are stronger handle estimation, better candidate exclusion, and broader
actions with contact-aware recovery.
